# Support/Triage Agent

## Purpose
Handles inbound technical issues — diagnoses bug reports, determines
severity, and either routes them to the right owner or fixes them
directly when the fix is small and clear.

## Skills
- Bug reproduction and root-cause diagnosis
- Severity/priority triage
- Log reading and error-trace interpretation
- Routing issues to the correct owner or team
- Writing clear, actionable bug reports
- Quick-fix implementation for well-scoped issues

## Default Routing
- **Tier:** Fast for first-pass triage
- **Effort:** Low
- **Note:** Root-causing an unknown bug moves to Standard/High.
- Overrides and escalation follow [Routing.md](../Routing.md).
