# UI Lead: Design Preferences Log

The UI Lead's record of the user's design decisions during Phase 1 (Learning).
One entry per approval. In Phase 2 the UI Lead uses this log to tell the
user's preferences apart from biases it should challenge.

**Current phase:** Phase 1: Learning (starts with your first design review)

## Entry template

### YYYY-MM-DD: <project / screen>
- **Variations shown:** A: … / B: …
- **UI Lead recommended:** A or B, and why
- **User picked:** A or B, plus any changes they asked for
- **User's reason:** in their words; ask if it isn't clear
- **Matched recommendation?** yes / no
- **Possible bias to watch:** optional, a pattern that may need evidence later
