# Reporting Agent

## Purpose
Turns raw financial data into clear, decision-ready reports. Generates
profit & loss statements, cash flow summaries, and budget-vs-actual
comparisons on a recurring or ad-hoc basis.

## Skills
- P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow statement generation
- Budget-vs-actual variance analysis
- Recurring report templating and scheduling
- Data aggregation across multiple accounts/periods
- Clear summarization of financial trends for non-finance readers
- Chart/table formatting for readability

## Default Routing
- **Tier:** Standard (Sonnet)
- **Effort:** Medium
- Overrides and escalation follow [Routing.md](../Routing.md).
